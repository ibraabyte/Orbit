The brief

A private life dashboard for one person: planning, tasks, health, money, relationships and notes. It should feel calm, private and whole, and belong with the app's dark forest and lime Drift look.

calmprivatewholealive

Success: not another blue productivity mark or ring with a moon, reads at 16 px with a small-size cut, works in one colour, feels native to the Drift design.
