# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Orbit is for a single owner who wants one reliable place to organize daily life across health, food planning, groceries, sleep/recovery, money, people and relationship follow-ups, focus time, tasks, reminders, reviews, and saved internet material. The user is usually checking the app quickly during the day, logging actions after they happen, or reviewing progress at the start and end of a day.

## Product Purpose

Orbit exists to be a personal command center: a focused PWA that combines today’s plan, focus blocks, people follow-ups and birthdays, review loops, calendar export, food plans and groceries, health and recovery logs, spending and bills, reliable reminders, and a searchable library for captured links/screenshots without turning into an overbuilt productivity suite. Success means the dashboard is useful every day, reminder notifications reach the user from every task entry path, the capture flow is fast enough to use in the moment, saved social/bookmark material can be found again, relationships do not disappear from view, reviews help close the loop, and personal data stays private under authenticated cloud accounts.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, personal. Orbit should feel like a quiet control room for life admin: polished, trustworthy, and efficient without looking corporate or fitness-bro.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing landing-page composition, gamified habit apps with noisy streak pressure, over-decorated dashboards, generic purple AI SaaS styling, and Notion-style blank-canvas ambiguity for core daily workflows.

## Design Principles

- Today first: the home screen should answer what needs attention now, including due standalone reminders and task reminder times.
- Close the loop: planning, logging, and review should reinforce each other.
- Show momentum without pressure: routines should make streaks visible without turning the product into a game.
- Capture without friction: saving a link, note, or screenshot must take seconds.
- Manual before magical: V1 uses reliable direct entry instead of fragile AI or third-party imports.
- Private by default: every record is scoped to the signed-in user and export/delete controls are visible.
- Dense but calm: repeated daily use matters more than spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard accessibility. Support reduced motion, clear focus states, non-color-only status cues, and graceful behavior when web push notifications are denied or unsupported.
